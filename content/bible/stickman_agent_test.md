# Kindled Iron, Stickman Animation Agent test

This is test #1 for the new visual direction.

Repository under test:
https://github.com/chrisaswain/stickman-animation-agent

The upstream project uses deterministic SVG and GSAP characters, structured Scene Definition JSON, Kokoro TTS, timestamps, camera movement, subtitles, and HyperFrames rendering. It supports a whiteboard template. citeturn0search0turn0search1

## Exact visual brief

Create a 30 to 45 second vertical 9:16 Kindled Iron test.

Topic: Genesis 1.

Narration:
"The Bible doesn't begin with Adam. It begins with God. Genesis 1 gives us the opening movement of creation, darkness, light, sky, land, plants, stars, sea life, animals, and finally humanity made in God's image. The point is not a verse by verse reading. It is the big picture, God creates an ordered world and gives humanity a unique place in it."

End card:
"READ IT YOURSELF: GENESIS 1"

Visual rules:
* off white whiteboard background
* dark black or charcoal ink
* burnt orange only for emphasis
* no stock footage
* no visible hand
* no pencil or marker
* no hand cursor
* drawings reveal themselves automatically
* stickman characters must actually move and act
* use walking, pointing, reacting, looking, reaching, and simple object interaction
* camera pans and zooms between major beats
* objects, arrows, symbols, and keywords reveal progressively
* keep compositions clean enough for a phone screen
* no verse by verse Scripture text
* no claims outside Genesis 1

## Success criteria

The rendered MP4 must demonstrate that the agent can produce:
1. moving stickman characters
2. progressive self drawing
3. camera movement
4. narration synchronized animation
5. captions
6. vertical 9:16 composition
7. a coherent Genesis 1 visual summary

Do not publish anything. This is strictly a local artifact test.
