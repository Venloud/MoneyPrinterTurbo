# Kindled Iron CoAnimator test

Target style:
* off white whiteboard background
* dark ink stick figures
* burnt orange emphasis
* no visible hand
* no pencil or marker hand
* self drawing line reveals
* actual stick figure movement and poses
* camera pans and zooms
* narration driven timing
* captions
* 9:16 output

Create a 30 to 45 second vertical 9:16 whiteboard explainer for Kindled Iron.
Do not use stock footage. Do not show a drawing hand, pencil, marker, or hand cursor.
Use an off white board, simple dark ink stick figures and objects, with burnt orange
accents only for emphasis. Draw every object progressively by itself. Stick figures
must move, walk, point, react, and perform actions through pose animation.
Use camera pans and zooms between scenes.

Narration concept:
"The Bible doesn't begin with Adam. It begins with God. Genesis 1 gives us the
opening movement of creation, darkness, light, sky, land, plants, stars, sea life,
animals, and finally humanity made in God's image. The point is not a verse by verse
reading. It is the big picture, God creates an ordered world and gives humanity a
unique place in it."

End card: READ IT YOURSELF: GENESIS 1

Keep every biblical claim grounded only in Genesis 1.

Save the resulting CoAnimator project in this directory as:
project/

The project should contain project.json.

After creating the project, the test renderer invokes:
python scripts/coanimator_render.py --project content/bible/coanimator/project --output storage/bible/coanimator/episode_001.mp4

The free CoAnimator tier is sufficient for this test. It renders locally at 720p
with a watermark. A paid licence is not required for this experiment.
